What is TomAI?

TomAI was created as my final project for CS50. Its purpose is to help users keep their medication schedules organized in one place through a simple and accessible dashboard.

The current version focuses on demonstrating the application's interface and its main medication-management features.

This project is an organizational tool, not a substitute for medical care. It does not provide diagnoses, prescribe medication, or determine dosages. Users should always follow the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional.
