About the project

TomAI

TomAI is the final project I made for CS50. The idea is to help people keep their medication routine organized. Users can save their medication schedules and record what happened with each dose during the day.

TomAI does not diagnose health conditions, recommend medications, or decide dosages. Always follow the instructions given by a qualified health professional.
